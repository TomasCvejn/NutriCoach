# Privacy policy

This text is a draft your coach will adapt to their practice. It describes what data the NutriCoach app processes and why.

## Who processes your data

The controller of your personal data is the coach who invited you to the app. They run it as a tool for nutrition coaching.

## What data we process

- Account: your name, email and the Google account you sign in with.
- Profile: age, sex, height, weight, goals, food preferences, allergies and health restrictions you enter yourself.
- Records: meals, portions, drinks, training sessions, body measurements, check-ins, competitions, supplements and messages with your coach.
- Health data (such as allergies, health conditions or body measurements) is a special category of data; we process it only with your explicit consent, which you give by entering it.

## Why we process it

- So that you and your coach can prepare your nutrition plan and follow your progress (performing the coaching agreement).
- To remind you of check-ins, messages and plan changes.

We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell it.

## Who receives it

- Your coach, who sees everything you enter; your coach's notes about you are visible to them only.
- The hosting provider the app runs on, and Google when you sign in.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you work with your coach. If you delete your account, we remove all your data without undue delay.

## Your rights

- Access and portability: download all your data in Settings.
- Erasure: delete your account and all its data in Settings.
- Rectification, restriction, objection and withdrawing consent – write to your coach.
- You can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

## Contact

For questions about your data, write to your coach through the app's messages.
