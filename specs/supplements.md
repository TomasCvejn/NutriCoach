# Supplements

- Supplement: name, product / brand (optional), category `DAILY` / `TRAINING` / `COMPETITION`, dose (text, e.g. "3 g"), timing (text, e.g. "30 min before training"), purpose, notes, active from / to.
- Coach assigns. The client can add supplements they take on their own (marked "added by client" for the coach).
- Supplement dashboard: current supplements grouped by category; tap → individual supplement screen (dose, timing, purpose, notes).
- Race-day schedule items can link to a supplement (`competition.md`).
