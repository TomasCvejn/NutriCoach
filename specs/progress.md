# Progress & monitoring

## Progress dashboard (client and coach)
Range 4 / 12 / 26 weeks. Sections:
- Nutrition: weekly adherence % (by tracking mode, `nutrition.md`); CALORIES also weekly avg kcal and protein vs. target.
- Performance (when Training is shown): weekly training minutes, avg RPE and "felt" score; competition results.
- Energy, recovery, mood, hunger, digestion: weekly check-in scores over time.
- Body composition: weight and body-fat trend.
Charts with Recharts; every chart has a table toggle.

## Body composition (occasional, e.g. monthly)
- Entry (client or coach): date, method (scale, bioimpedance, DEXA, skinfolds, tape), weight kg, body fat %, lean mass kg (computed `weight × (1 − bf%)` when not entered), measurements cm (waist, hips, chest, thigh, arm), note.
- Graphs over time + table with Δ vs. the previous entry.

## Bloodwork (optional for the client)
- Test: date, lab (optional), attached documents (pdf/jpg/png), markers: name, value, unit, optional reference range.
- Preset markers: hemoglobin, hematocrit, ferritin, iron, transferrin saturation, vitamin D (25-OH), vitamin B12, folate, TSH, fT4, CRP, glucose, HbA1c, total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides, creatinine, magnesium, zinc — plus custom markers.
- Out-of-range values highlighted. History per marker across tests (chart + table).
- Client or coach adds. Included in data export and account deletion.
